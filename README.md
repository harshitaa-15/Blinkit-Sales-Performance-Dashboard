

# Blinkit Grocery Sales Analysis 

![Blinkit Dashboard](Images/Blinkit-MainDashboard.png)

## Executive Summary

This project presents an end-to-end analysis of Blinkit grocery sales data using SQL and Power BI. The objective is to transform raw retail data into meaningful business insights by analyzing sales performance, product categories, outlet characteristics, customer ratings, and item visibility.

SQL was used for data cleaning, transformation, aggregation, and exploratory analysis, while Power BI was used to develop an interactive dashboard for business reporting and decision-making.

The project follows a structured analytical workflow:

**Business Requirements → Data Preparation → SQL Analysis → KPI Development → Data Visualization → Business Insights**

---

## Business Problem

Retail businesses need to understand the factors influencing sales performance across products and outlets. This project addresses key business questions related to product performance, outlet characteristics, customer satisfaction, and sales distribution.

The analysis focuses on:

- Overall sales performance
- Sales by product category
- Sales by item fat content
- Sales by outlet location
- Sales contribution by outlet size
- Sales by outlet establishment year
- Outlet type performance
- Customer ratings
- Item visibility
- Number of items

## Dataset

The dataset contains grocery product and outlet-level information required to analyze Blinkit's retail sales performance.

### Key Columns

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| `Item_Identifier` | Unique identifier for each product |
| `Item_Type` | Product category |
| `Item_Fat_Content` | Fat-content classification of the product |
| `Item_Weight` | Weight of the product |
| `Item_Visibility` | Visibility of the product within the outlet |
| `Total_Sales` | Sales generated by the product |
| `Rating` | Customer rating |
| `Outlet_Identifier` | Unique identifier for each outlet |
| `Outlet_Establishment_Year` | Year the outlet was established |
| `Outlet_Size` | Size classification of the outlet |
| `Outlet_Location_Type` | Outlet location tier |
| `Outlet_Type` | Type of outlet |

---

## Data Preparation

Before analysis, the dataset was reviewed for consistency and categorical standardization.

The `Item_Fat_Content` column contained multiple representations of the same categories, including:

- `LF`
- `low fat`
- `Low Fat`
- `reg`
- `Regular`

These values were standardized into two consistent categories:

- `Low Fat`
- `Regular`

## Key Insights

### Overall Sales

The dataset records approximately **$1.20M in total sales** across **8,523 items**, with an average sales value of **$141**.

### Product Categories

**Fruits & Vegetables** and **Snack Foods** are among the highest-performing product categories based on total sales.

### Fat Content

**Low Fat** products contribute a larger share of total sales compared with Regular products in the analyzed dataset.

### Outlet Location

**Tier 3 outlets** generate the highest total sales among the three outlet location categories.

### Outlet Size

**Medium-sized outlets** contribute the largest share of total sales, followed by Small and High-sized outlets.

### Outlet Type

**Supermarket Type1** generates the highest total sales among the outlet types analyzed.

### Customer Satisfaction

Average ratings across outlet types remain relatively consistent, with the overall average rating standing at **3.9**.

### Item Visibility

Item visibility is analyzed alongside sales and outlet performance to provide additional context around product exposure.
